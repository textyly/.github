# textyly

Open-source and community-driven projects for textile arts.

Textile crafts such as embroidery, knitting and crochet are part of humanity's intangible cultural heritage, and they are still practised today. textyly builds free software to design, share and preserve them.

## Projects

### [Crossly](https://github.com/textyly/crossly)

An editor for designing and sharing cross-stitch patterns. Instead of painting squares on a grid, you connect dots that stand for needle punctures, so a pattern records how the thread really travels through the fabric, and the editor can show the front and the back of the work side by side. Crossly is built to help preserve traditional Bulgarian embroidery as digital cultural heritage.

*Status: early development.* See the [plan](https://github.com/textyly/crossly/blob/main/PLAN.md) for what exists and what comes next.
